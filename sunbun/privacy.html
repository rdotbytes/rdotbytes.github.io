Privacy Policy for Sunbun

Effective from Sunbun's first release · Last updated 25 October 2026

Sunbun cannot send anything anywhere. It has no permission to use the internet, so nothing about you, your alarms or your sleep ever leaves your phone.

Sunbun is an alarm clock with sleep sounds, made by rdotbytes. This page explains what it keeps and why. The short version: everything stays on your phone, and uninstalling Sunbun removes it.

What Sunbun collects about you

Nothing. Sunbun has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no advertising identifier. It does not know who you are and has no way to find out.

This is not only a promise. Android lets an app reach the internet only if it asks for that permission when it is installed, and Sunbun does not ask. You can see this yourself in your phone's settings, under Sunbun's permissions.

What Sunbun keeps on your phone

To do its job, Sunbun keeps a few small files in its own private storage on your device. Android prevents other apps from reading them.

  • Your alarms — their times, days, names, sounds and settings.
  • Your settings, and the sleep sounds you played last, so the Play button can bring them back.
  • A record of each alarm ringing and each time the sleep sounds play — when, which sound, how it stopped, and the phone's battery level at the time. It is there so a missed alarm can be explained. It never leaves the phone; a computer can read it only when connected to the phone by a USB cable with Android's developer settings switched on.
  • The date Sunbun was installed. It is kept so that if a paid extra is ever added, nobody who already has a feature loses it. It is never sent anywhere either.

Some of this is kept where your phone can read it before you unlock it after a restart. That is deliberate: it is how an alarm still rings if your phone restarts in the night. It is still private to Sunbun.

Backups

Sunbun switches off Android's own backup for itself, so your alarms are not copied to Google's cloud or to a new phone. The cost is honest: on a new phone you set your alarms again.

Why Sunbun asks for what it asks for

  • Alarms and reminders — to ring at the exact minute you chose.
  • Notifications and full-screen alarms — so a ringing alarm can wake your screen and show over the lock screen.
  • Running in the background, and after a restart — so an alarm rings even when Sunbun is closed, and after your phone restarts.
  • Keeping the phone awake and vibrating — while an alarm rings.
  • Playing media in the background — so sleep sounds keep playing with the screen off.

None of these involves sending anything off the phone.

Children

Sunbun is not directed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone of any age.

Deleting your information

Deleting an alarm in Sunbun deletes it. Uninstalling Sunbun deletes everything it holds, because all of it lives inside the app's own storage. There is no account to close and nothing to ask us to delete, because we never had any of it.

Changes to this policy

If Sunbun ever changes what it does with information, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Anything that meant sending information off the phone would be announced in the app before it happened.
